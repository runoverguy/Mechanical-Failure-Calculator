Distortion Energy Theory Breakdown

The Distortion Energy Theory (also known as the von Mises or Huber-Hencky theory) posits that yielding begins when the strain energy associated with shear deformation (distortion) equals the distortion strain energy at yield in simple tension.

$\sigma' = \sqrt{\frac{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2 + (\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2 + (\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2}{2}}$

For 2D Plane Stress ($\sigma_3 = 0$), this simplifies to:

$\sigma' = \sqrt{\sigma_x^2 - \sigma_x\sigma_y + \sigma_y^2 + 3\tau_{xy}^2}$
